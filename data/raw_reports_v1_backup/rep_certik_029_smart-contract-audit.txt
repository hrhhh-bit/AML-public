What’s in a Smart Contract Audit Report?
Audit reports are comprehensive, fully transparent records that detail all identified vulnerabilities. These are classified by severity from Critical to Informational. Suggested remediation from our team of smart contract security experts accompany each vulnerability.
Vulnerabilities that are addressed before launch are marked as resolved. Issues acknowledged but unresolved are accompanied by a description of their potential effects and the project team’s justification.
