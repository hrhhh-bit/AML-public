What is Web3 penetration testing?
Compared to Web2, Web3 brings unique challenges. Web3 penetration testers must understand blockchain technology, smart contracts, key management, on-chain/off-chain communication, and Web3-specific threats. Applications often combine decentralized contracts with centralized backend services and custody infrastructure. On top of that, financial regulations and compliance frameworks introduce additional requirements around system security, operational resilience, and data protection. Effective testing must account for both technical vulnerabilities and compliance-driven risks across the entire stack.

Penetration Testing Process
Our penetration testing workflow is optimized for the unique architecture, threat models, and attack surfaces of Web3 environments.
1. PREPARE
Define engagement parameters CertiK will issue a questionnaire to ensure alignment on scope, timeline, restrictions, limitations, and custom objectives.
2. EXPLORE
Reconnaissance and vulnerability analysis Our team will conduct reconnaissance to identify public-facing assets, followed by a vulnerability analysis to uncover potential risks.
3. EXPLOIT
Target Exploitation Our team will attempt to exploit vulnerabilities and bypass security controls using a variety of tools and tactics.
4. ESCALATE
Privilege Escalation and Lateral Movement Our pentesters will simulate attacker objectives by attempting to escalate privileges and access key material and sensitive data.
5. IMPROVE
Deliver Findings and Recommendations CertiK will deliver a detailed report outlining the potential business impact and providing specific, actionable recommendations.
